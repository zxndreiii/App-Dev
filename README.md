# Slam Dunk

> **Genre:** Sports, Comedy, Drama
>
> **Platform:** Netflix, Crunchyroll
>
> **Season:** 1

## Summary

Slam Dunk follows Hanamichi Sakuragi, a high school student who joins the Shohoku High School basketball team to impress a girl named Haruko Akagi. Although he initially has no experience in basketball, he gradually discovers his passion for the sport. Alongside his teammates, including the talented Kaede Rukawa, Sakuragi faces challenging opponents, learns teamwork, and works hard to help Shohoku become a strong basketball team.

## Main Character

1. Hanamichi Sakuragi
2. Kaede Rukawa
